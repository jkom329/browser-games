Practical revisions

  • Continuous circulation loop
  • Kitchen beside dining + family

Finish schedule

  • Full-height kitchen cabinetry

  • All mirrors beveled and framed

  • 7¼" baseboards; substantial door/window trim
  • Tall feature window at stair landing

Concept, not a permit set.
This drawing establishes room relationships, target dimensions, and area. A licensed architect and engineers must adapt it to the site, structure, energy code, egress, accessibility, and local permitting requirements before construction.

StructureConcept assumes steel at the open hall, engineered wood floor trusses, and reinforced concrete at the lower level.
EnvelopeTarget high-performance wall assembly with exterior continuous insulation; final R-values are climate-zone dependent.
Life safetyTwo enclosed stairs serve all levels. Egress windows, guards, smoke control, and fire separation require code review.

01 / KEEP THE DRAMA

The split stair stays.

The arrival sequence, double-height hall, bridge, and symmetrical stair are the emotional center of the house.

02 / REMOVE THE MAZE

Every room earns a route.

Public rooms connect in a clear loop. Service circulation is short, legible, and separate from the formal entry.

03 / CARRY THE CRAFT FORWARD

Light, pearl, and millwork.

Ceiling-height cabinetry, mother-of-pearl tile, wainscoting, crown molding, tall baseboards, framed beveled mirrors, and chandeliers form a consistent finish language.
