The bump-out rule

The bump-out is a machine bay, not a pile. The only things allowed on its floor are on wheels — snowblower and mower on the mat. Strollers go up on hooks; everything else goes to a wall, a shelf, or the ceiling.

Seasonal swap

Snowblower and mower trade the front spot twice a year — blower forward in November, mower forward in April. The off-season machine goes to the back of the bay, under its cover, so the swap takes two minutes.

Both bays stay empty

Two cars, every night. If something lands in a bay, the system failed somewhere — the walls, shelves and overhead rack are sized to absorb everything that currently hits the floor.
