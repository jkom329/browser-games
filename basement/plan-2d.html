Basement — simplified 2D plan

Drawn from sheet C7 (foundation plan, Veridian Maybeck Cottage, 1207 Wild Willow Way). North is up. 1 grid square = 2 ft. Dimensions in feet, measured from the northwest outer corner of the basement.

What this drawing is

  • Basement shell 34 × 40 ft, from the printed dimensions on C7. Front (north) wall has the bay bump-out; garage sits unexcavated to the east; the strip south of the basement is unexcavated.
  • Stairwell on the west side, roughly mid-depth (north wall ≈ 18 ft from the north wall of the basement). Two flights side by side, both running east–west: the north flight goes UP to the second floor, the south flight comes DN to the basement. You step off the DN flight at the west end, by the west wall.
  • Bathroom rough-in in the middle of the room, just east of the stairs: ≈ 7 × 7 ft, west wall ≈ 18 ft from the west wall. Lav and WC are drawn on the south side on C7; shower goes at the west end.
  • Mechanicals: furnace just north of the bathroom (≈ 23–26 ft from the west wall); two water heaters + softener in a column along the east wall (≈ 19–25 ft from the north wall). Sump in the southwest corner; 200A panel on the west wall south of the stairs.
  • Steel posts (K): two north–south beam lines at ≈ 10 ft and ≈ 26.5 ft from the west wall, posts near the north and south ends, plus one post just outside the bathroom's northwest corner and one just east of the bathroom's northeast corner.

What was wrong in the old 3D model

  • Stairs ran north–south along the west wall as a single flight. On C7 they run east–west as a double flight.
  • Bathroom sat at the west side (x ≈ 3.5–13 ft), overlapping where the stairwell actually is. On C7 it is in the middle of the room (x ≈ 18–25 ft).
  • Water heater, softener and furnace were all too far west and too far north; they belong along the east wall and just north of the bathroom.
  • Beams were at x ≈ 13 and 23 ft; the post lines on C7 sit at ≈ 10 and 26.5 ft. The old model also had six posts in a neat 2×3 grid; C7 has six posts but not in a grid.
  • The duct run was ≈ 25 ft because the bathroom was in the wrong place. From the corrected bathroom the fan-to-rim-joist run is ≈ 13 ft — the buy list drops from six 5-ft sections to three.
