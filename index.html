02 / Chess Engine

Furtificus

A chess engine I wrote that played on the Free Internet Chess Server (FICS) as the computer account leaderbeans(C), running on a 600 MHz Pentium with 161 MB of RAM. Active from 2008 to 2013 — it spent so much time online that by January 2011 it had already logged more than 400 days on the server.

9,600+hours online (400+ days by Jan 2011)
94,015games in the FICS database
1637Blitz rating (best 1742)

Rated blitz record: 501 wins, 213 losses, 73 draws. Rated lightning record: 164 wins, 102 losses, 18 draws. Of the 94,015 games in the database, 87,338 are blitz. Last build: October 7, 2008; last disconnected October 30, 2013.
